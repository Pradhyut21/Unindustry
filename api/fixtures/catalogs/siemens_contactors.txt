The Siemens 3RT2015 is a 3-pole contactor designed for motor control applications
in industrial automation. Suitable for switching motors up to 3 kW at 400V AC.
The snap-on auxiliary contact blocks allow for easy circuit expansion.
DIN rail mounting. Integrated surge suppressor option available.
